What to wear

Two looks each, from your own closet.

Please don't buy anything. These are suggestions, not rules. Think polished business casual: one look with a jacket, one without, so we get two feels in one morning. On color, don't overthink it. Solid colors you already love wearing are perfect. Just compare notes with each other before Thursday so the three of you don't show up in the same shade.

Look 1 · The advisor

Polished, with a jacket

Dana and Amy: a blazer or structured jacket over a simple solid top, or a solid dress with a jacket.

Adam: a sport coat or blazer with an open-collar shirt. No tie needed, but bring one if you love it.

This is your portrait and leadership look, so wear what you'd wear to a first meeting with your biggest client.

Look 2 · The partner

Same morning, jacket off

Dana and Amy: a fine-knit sweater, a cardigan or a soft blouse. Or simply Look 1 without the jacket.

Adam: a sweater over a collared shirt, or the shirt on its own with sleeves rolled.

This one's for the working shots, where you look like the people agency owners actually want across the table.

These help

  • Solid colors with some weight to the fabric, in your everyday favorites
  • Open necklines, like a soft V or a wide crew
  • Jewelry and watches you wear every day
  • A steamer and a lint roller

Skip these

  • All black, which disappears into the leather chairs
  • Tan and rust, which blend into the sofas
  • Busy prints, thin stripes, logos and shiny fabrics
  • Anything you'll tug at all morning
What to bring
  • A laptop or two with a sample dashboard or report open. Dummy or blurred numbers only; you're the stewards of your clients' data, and the photos should never show the real thing.
  • A printed sample report or two to hold and point at
  • The notebooks and pens you actually use with clients
  • Your favorite mugs, and anything branded you love
  • A photo release for Dell, and for anyone else in the photos
